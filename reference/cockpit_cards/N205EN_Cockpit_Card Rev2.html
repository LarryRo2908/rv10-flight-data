3. Operational Rules of Thumb
🎯 Standard Climb Target (OAT < 80°F): At 1,000 ft AGL, adjust mixture directly to 18.0 GPH / ~1,325°F Cyl #4 EGT (~105°F ROP). Target airspeed: 120–125 KIAS.
🔥 Hot Day Override (OAT > 80°F): Maintain ≥ 19.5 GPH (Full Rich) until above 3,000 ft MSL and pitch for 125–130 KIAS to ensure CHTs remain < 380°F.
📉 Altitude Leaning Schedule: Turn mixture knob lean by ~1.0 GPH (~1 full turn) per 2,000 ft of climb up to top of climb.
⚡ Thermal Safety Trigger: If any CHT touches 380°F during climb, lower nose for +5 kt IAS and enrich mixture by +1.0 GPH.
